Homemade Lasagna

Homemade Lasagna isnt hard to make as it seems. While the layers may look impressive, the process is simple and comes together step by step. Once you try this recipe, you will never want to order lasagna from a restaurant again.

Ingredients

  • Meat
  • Onion
  • Canned tomatoes
  • Sugar
  • Noodles
  1. Gather all ingredients
  2. Combine pork and ground beef in a large, deep skillet over medium-high heat; cook and stir until browned and crumbly, 5 to 7 minutes
  3. Add onion and cook until translucent, about 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato sauce, 1 tablespoon fresh parsley, garlic, basil, salt, oregano, and sugar. Reduce heat to medium-low and simmer, stirring occasionally, for 30 minutes.
  5. Remove from the oven and let stand for 10 minutes before cutting and serving
